Why We Started

A story born from a simple question

We are a small group of people from Kotagiri — teachers, farmers, and ordinary neighbours — who kept asking ourselves: why do we wait for someone else to help?

We saw need around us. Not the dramatic kind you see on TV, but the quiet kind — a child missing school because of illness, a family unable to afford medicine, an elder without someone to talk to.

So we did the only thing we could. We started. Not with a big budget or a grand plan, but with genuine intention and whatever we could gather.

"We may be small today. But every tree you see was once a seed no one noticed."
Our First Step

It may be one life today…

We won't pretend we've changed the world. We haven't. But we helped one person — a young student in our village who needed support to continue her education.

It cost less than what most of us spend on a month's phone bill. But to her family, it meant everything. That moment is why this trust exists.
